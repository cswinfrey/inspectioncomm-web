INSPECTIONCOMM FIELD GUIDE
Internal Reference for Inspectors & Managers
Last revised: August 2026
================================================================

How to run an inspection start to finish in the InspectionComm app,
and what managers need to keep the roster and incoming requests moving.


PART ONE -- INSPECTORS
================================================================

1. GETTING STARTED
----------------------------------------------------------------
A manager creates your account and gives you a temporary password
directly -- there's no public sign-up. The first time you log in,
the app forces you to replace it before you can do anything else.

  1. Go to /inspector/login and sign in with the email and
     temporary password your manager gave you.
  2. You'll land on "Change Password" automatically -- this screen
     won't let you through until you set your own password.
  3. From then on, log in normally at /inspector/login any time.

  NOTE: Forgot your password? There's no self-service reset yet --
  ask a manager to set you a new temporary one from the roster.


2. YOUR DASHBOARD
----------------------------------------------------------------
After logging in you see YOUR OWN inspections only -- not the whole
team's. Each row shows the vehicle, customer, date, and status.

  - Search by vehicle, VIN, or customer name using the search box.
  - Filter by status (In progress / Completed) and by date range.
  - "New Inspection" starts a fresh one; if you're a manager,
    "Manager View" also appears here.


3. STARTING AN INSPECTION
----------------------------------------------------------------
Click "New Inspection" and fill in what you know up front -- you
can always finish the details on the checklist screen afterward.

  1. Enter the customer's name and email. If they've been inspected
     before, start typing and pick them from the suggestions
     instead of creating a duplicate.
  2. Enter what you have on the vehicle: VIN, year, mileage, make
     and model are required; color and plate can wait if you don't
     have them yet.
  3. Set the inspection date and type, then click "Create Inspection".

This takes you straight to the inspection's detail page, where the
checklist and photo upload live.


4. THE CHECKLIST
----------------------------------------------------------------
The checklist is organized into the same sections you'd walk
through on the vehicle itself. Every condition field has the same
four options:

    Good | Fair | Poor | Not Applicable

Use "Not Applicable" rather than leaving a field blank when
something genuinely doesn't apply to this vehicle -- a blank field
reads as "not checked," which is a different claim than "doesn't
apply here."

  Section                   | Covers
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  Vehicle identity          | Color, license plate & state
  Odometer                  | Reading before and after the
                             | inspection
  Engine & transmission     | Fuel type (Gas / Diesel / Electric /
                             | Other), engine size & cylinders,
                             | transmission type & condition
  Tires                     | Size, tread, condition
  Exterior & steering       | Paint, suspension & steering, power
                             | steering type & condition
  Fluids & climate          | Brake fluid level & condition, fluid
                             | leaks (with notes), A/C & heat
  Interior electronics      | Radio, heated/cooled seats, sunroof,
                             | rear tailgate
  OBD scan                  | ECM, TCM, ABS, SRS, AWD/4WD -- Pass,
                             | Codes Found, or Not Applicable
  Notes & synopsis          | Your free-form notes, plus the
                             | overall synopsis that becomes your
                             | diagnosis on the customer's report

For fuel type, pick "Other" from the dropdown to get a text box if
it's not gas, diesel, or electric.

Click "Save Checklist" any time -- you don't have to finish in one
sitting. Come back and keep editing as long as the inspection is
still "In Progress."

  CAUTION: Once an inspection is marked "Completed," the checklist
  locks for you. Only a manager can make further corrections -- see
  section 10, "Correcting a completed report." Don't mark it
  complete until you're actually done.


5. PHOTOS & VIDEO
----------------------------------------------------------------
Scroll to "Photos & video" on the inspection page to upload
evidence for what you found.

  1. Type a tag describing what the batch documents -- start typing
     for suggestions like "Tires," "Engine," "Brakes / Fluids," or
     write something more specific like "Left front tire."
  2. Choose one or several files at once -- the whole batch gets
     that tag.
  3. Each file uploads with its own progress indicator; if one
     fails, retry just that file without re-selecting the others.

Photos group automatically by tag on the page and on the customer's
report, so related shots stay together instead of one long
unsorted strip. You can edit any photo's tag afterward by clicking
it -- useful for turning a batch tag like "Tires" into something
more specific once you know which wheel it was.


6. MARKING COMPLETE
----------------------------------------------------------------
When the checklist and photos are done, use the "Mark Complete"
button near the top of the inspection page.

  - Status changes to "Completed" and the report becomes final from
    your side.
  - You can reopen it yourself with "Reopen" if you catch something
    right away -- but once you navigate off and come back, further
    edits need a manager (see above).


7. SHARING THE REPORT
----------------------------------------------------------------
Every inspection has a "Copy Report Link" button near the top of
its page. That link is the entire access model -- anyone who has it
can view that one report, no login required. Text or email it
straight to the customer.

The report page shows the checklist, all tagged photos, and the
completing inspector's name -- it's read-only regardless of status.


PART TWO -- MANAGERS
================================================================

8. MANAGING THE ROSTER
----------------------------------------------------------------
Open "Manager View" from your dashboard.

  Adding an inspector:
    1. Enter their name, email, and a temporary password
       (8+ characters) -- anything you choose, since they'll
       replace it on first login.
    2. Tell them the temp password directly. There's no automated
       invite email.

  Deactivating an inspector:
    Click "Deactivate" next to their name. This cuts off their
    access immediately -- both future logins and any session
    they're already in. Use "Reactivate" to restore it later. You
    can't deactivate your own account from here.


9. MANAGER VIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------
Manager View shows the whole team's activity, not just your own:

  - Inspections this month and total, at a glance.
  - A per-inspector count, so you can see who's carrying the load.
  - Every inspection across the team, with the same
    search/status/date filters as the personal dashboard.


10. CORRECTING A COMPLETED REPORT
----------------------------------------------------------------
Managers aren't locked out once an inspection is marked complete.
Open it from Manager View like any other -- the checklist, notes,
synopsis, and photo tags are all still editable for you, so you can
fix a mistake after the fact without asking the original inspector
to reopen it.


11. TRIAGING INSPECTION REQUESTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
The "Request an Inspection" form on the public website lands in
"Requests," linked from the dashboard -- this is the team's shared
lead inbox, visible to every inspector, not manager-only.

Each request shows the requestor's contact info, vehicle, and any
notes they left. Move it through a status as you work it:

    New -> Contacted -> Scheduled -> Closed

Once you've scheduled it, create the actual inspection the normal
way from "New Inspection" -- the request and the inspection aren't
linked automatically, so carry the vehicle and contact details over
yourself.
